Objective

Grow Burberry's rank in the female fragrance category in a market that barely bought female fragrance.

Insight

India is a male-dominated fragrance market. Women weren't under-served by product — they were under-served by occasion.

Idea

A 30×30 ft immersive experience, powered by a PR event with Sephora Beauty, that made the fragrance a destination rather than a shelf.

Execution

110 points of sale, pop-ups and outdoor, homepage and category ads on Nykaa, Sephora and Shoppers Stop, affinity-based Google and YouTube video, and the top 100 beauty influencers.

05 About

Mumbai → Milan → New York

Over eight years I've worked with a Fortune 500 media house, with startups, and led global consumer luxury brands. The through-line is that I like being close to both ends of the work — the idea and the invoice.

I'm currently doing my MS in Marketing Analytics at Baruch College's Zicklin School of Business. Before that, an MBA at SDA Bocconi and an undergraduate degree in accounting and finance from the University of Mumbai. The accounting background is why I'm comfortable with a P&L; the Bocconi years are why I'm comfortable being the only person in the room who hasn't been there before.

My view is that surrounding yourself with people who have the right attitude matters more than surrounding yourself with people who have the right skills. Skills are teachable on a deadline. Attitude isn't.

Outside work I experiment with cooking and escape into nature. I've tried ten coffee chains in the last three months, and I'm still counting.
